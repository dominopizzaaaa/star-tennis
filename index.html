Private lessons can be held anywhere in Singapore — at your condo, club court or a nearby public court. Join-a-Group sessions run at selected public courts.

Tell us your goals, level, preferred format and availability on WhatsApp. We will recommend the best route, from individual coaching to a matched group.

Wear sports attire and non-marking tennis shoes. Bring a racket, water, towel, cap and sun protection. New player without a racket? Let us know before your first lesson.

Complete our short registration form or message us on WhatsApp for the fastest reply. We will help with level, venue and scheduling.
